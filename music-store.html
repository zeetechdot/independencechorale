How to sell PDFs without building a database

The easiest setup is to use a payment link from your preferred payment provider. After successful payment, the buyer can be redirected to a protected download page or the PDF can be delivered by email.

  1. Upload each PDF to your hosting or cloud storage.
  2. Create a payment link for each music score.
  3. Replace the “Request / Buy” button with the payment link.
  4. Deliver the file automatically after payment or by email.
Do not place paid PDF files in a publicly linked folder if you want to restrict unpaid downloads.

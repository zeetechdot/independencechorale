About us

A choir built on passion, discipline and community.

Independence Chorale started in 2021 when a few passionate singers came together to explore the world of choral music. Through support and partnership, Independence Chorale Abuja was formed.

We continue to create opportunities for singers and instrumentalists to learn, perform and share memorable musical experiences.
